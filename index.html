How an order runs

Eight to ten weeks, start to finish.

The clock starts when the drawing is approved, not when the PO arrives. Here is where the time actually goes.

  1. Day 0–1

    Drawing review & quote

    We check feasibility, tooling needs and tolerance. You get a priced offer, not a "contact us".

  2. Day 1–5

    Technical confirmation

    Material grade, coating spec, marking, packaging. Any drawing correction happens here, before tooling.

  3. Week 1–3

    Tooling & samples

    Heading dies and thread rolls made; first-article samples shipped for your approval.

  4. Week 3–8

    Mass production

    Cold heading, threading, heat treatment, coating. In-process checks at each stage.

  5. Week 8–9

    Inspection & reports

    Tensile, salt spray and dimensional testing. Reports issued with the lot, before dispatch.

  6. Week 9–10

    Packing & delivery

    Export packing, documentation, DDP delivery to your door.

Before you ask

The practical questions.

What is the minimum order?

3,000 pieces per size and finish, and 50,000 pieces is the practical ceiling for a single order. Below 3,000 the heading dies and thread-roll setup dominate the price — we will still quote it, but it is rarely worth it for either side.

Can I mix sizes in one order?

Yes, but each size and finish is priced on its own quantity. 3,000 pcs of M14 is one line; 1,500 M14 plus 1,500 M12 is two lines and does not reach either minimum.

What files do you need?

STEP or IGES if you have 3D. DXF/DWG or a dimensioned PDF if 2D. A physical sample also works — we reverse-engineer it and send the drawing back for your approval before cutting tooling.

Who owns the tooling?

You do, once it is paid for. It stays in our plant for your repeat orders and is not used for anyone else's parts.

Do you supply standard, off-the-shelf bolts?

No. We only run custom work. If you need catalogue parts, a distributor will serve you faster and cheaper.

Can you ship DDP to my door?

Yes — to the US, EU, UK and UAE. Duties and delivery are included in the price, or quoted separately if you prefer to clear customs yourself. On FOB and CIF we confirm the loading port with you; Ningbo is our default.

What are the payment terms?

30% with the order, 70% against the pre-shipment inspection report. Terms are negotiable after the first two orders.

Do you handle private label?

Yes. Head marking, laser marking, retail boxes and inserts can all carry your brand.
